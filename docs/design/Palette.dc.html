This column is the argument. No color in this palette — or any palette — survives naked on the photosphere. Region labels are gold text sitting exactly here today, at 1.54:1. Legibility over the Sun has to come from a plate or a casing, never from a color choice.

Guard-rail on --sol-ember

Ember sits at hue 340°. The inbound-field orange is at 20° and the X-flare marker at 6° — under deuteranopia those three converge. So ember is restricted to three places where it is never adjacent to data and never a small dot:

  • the 1px gradient rule under the top bar
  • the section-header rules in the rail
  • the kiosk “Take it with you” pill

Never a status, never a severity, never a legend swatch.

Typography
Pirulenwordmark + display. 20px and up only — a wide techno face muddies below that
Magistralall UI: labels, buttons, stat values, and section heads at 0.22em tracking — the kit’s own convention
Omnesbody copy at small sizes — exactly what the kit specifies it for

Licensing is unresolved and blocking. None of the three is on Google Fonts. Pirulen (Typodermic) is free for personal use only; Magistral (ParaType) and Omnes (Darden / Adobe Fonts) need webfont licenses. The app already bundles local TTFs, so the mechanism exists — the rights do not yet. Until they do, ship this stack, which is what these artboards are drawn in:

--sol-font-display: "Pirulen", "Chakra Petch", "Highway Gothic Narrow", sans-serif;
--sol-font-ui: "Magistral", "Barlow", system-ui, sans-serif;
--sol-font-body: "Omnes", "Nunito Sans", system-ui, sans-serif;

Every live number carries font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so stat values stop jittering as they refresh.
